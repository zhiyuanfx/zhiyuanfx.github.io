## Research overview

The work explores block-wise power sampling and token-wise inverse conditional probabilities for choosing suffix tokens to resample. The objective is to improve reasoning accuracy while reducing computation.

My résumé reports 35% less computation time and 10% higher single-shot accuracy on MATH500 for the block-wise power-sampling implementation compared with its original version.
