## Research overview

Advised by Prof. Chaoyue Zhao and Prof. Lei Fan, I studied QHD for non-smooth, non-convex optimization. The work combines QHD with Sequential Quadratic Programming and automated experiments across more than 50 benchmark functions and 2,000 test instances.

## Publication

**Quantum Hamiltonian Descent for Non-smooth Optimization**

Jiaqi Leng, Yufan Zheng, Zhiyuan Jia, Lei Fan, Chaoyue Zhao, et al. (2025).

[Read the preprint on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15878).
